# Dashboard Keuangan

URL: `sales-dashboard-keuangan`

Financial-focused dashboard (separate from POS dashboard).

## §1 KPI cards

- Pendapatan bulan ini (revenue MTD)
- Beban bulan ini (expense MTD)
- Laba kotor (gross profit MTD)
- Saldo Kas (current cash balance, all accounts)
- Hutang outstanding (AP)
- Piutang outstanding (AR)
- Aset tetap (fixed asset NBV)

## §2 Charts

- Revenue vs expense trend (last 12 months)
- Cash flow waterfall (current month)
- Expense breakdown pie (current month)
- AR aging bar
- AP aging bar

## §3 Quick links

- Tutup Buku (close month)
- Lihat Buku Besar
- Tambah Pengeluaran
- Tambah Penerimaan

## §4 Mobile considerations

- Owner-only view.
- Tablet primary surface.
- Phone: simplified, fewer charts.
- Refresh: pull-to-refresh.
